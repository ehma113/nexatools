Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit NexaTools, use its tools or contact the site, including the role of browser processing and external service providers.

Information this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled when you visit NexaTools, browse its pages, use its online utilities or contact the site by email. It describes the current static website and the types of information that may be involved. A particular tool or a third-party service may have additional notices or terms that apply to that feature.

Information you enter into tools

NexaTools is designed so that many calculations, text operations and file utilities run directly in your browser. When a tool works locally, the text, numbers or selected file are processed by the code running on your device and are not intentionally sent to a NexaTools account database. This website version does not require you to create an account to use its tools. However, some tools may contact an external provider to obtain data or load a supporting library. For example, the weather feature requests location-related forecast information from external weather services when you use it. The exact data flow can vary by tool, so check the description of the feature you are using. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, confidential business records or other sensitive information to a tool unless you have independently confirmed that it is appropriate.

Server, hosting and security information

When your browser requests a page or file, the hosting, content-delivery or security provider used to operate the website may process technical information. Depending on the provider and configuration, this can include an IP address, request date and time, requested page or file, browser and device information, approximate network or region information, and security or error logs. These records are generally used to deliver the website, diagnose faults, prevent abuse and maintain security. NexaTools does not currently specify a fixed log-retention period because that depends on the hosting and security services configured for the live site. The relevant provider’s privacy notice and retention terms also apply.

Emails and messages you send

If you contact NexaTools at mathsolverpro1@gmail.com, the email provider and the site operator will receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, attachments and any details needed to respond. Your message may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain support records, address a reported issue or meet applicable obligations. Please do not email passwords, payment details or sensitive documents. Email is not guaranteed to be a secure channel for confidential information.

Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The website code supplied in this release does not include a configured analytics or advertising tag. Some browser features or third-party services loaded by a page may nevertheless use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for essential operation, preferences, security or service delivery. Your browser settings may allow you to view, block or delete stored data, although blocking it can affect how some features work. If NexaTools adds analytics, advertising, personalisation or other optional tracking in the future, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated to describe those services before or when they are enabled.

Third-party libraries, APIs and external websites

Some features depend on code, fonts, APIs or data supplied by other organisations. When your browser connects to those providers, they may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser details and the specific resource requested. The weather feature, for instance, uses external geocoding and forecast services. Links to external websites may also take you away from NexaTools. These providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms, and NexaTools does not control their systems or retention practices. Review their notices before using a third-party feature or following an external link.

Advertising and measurement

Advertising and analytics are not represented as active in the website source reviewed for this update. If advertising services such as Google AdSense or analytics tools are introduced, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure activity, prevent invalid traffic, limit repeated adverts or deliver and measure advertisements, subject to their own policies and the choices or consent mechanisms required in your region. This policy must be reviewed again when those services are actually configured. Do not assume that an advertising or analytics service is active merely because it is mentioned here.

How information may be used

Information described in this policy may be used to operate and display the website, provide the tool or external data you request, respond to your emails, investigate technical problems, protect the site against misuse, and comply with applicable legal requirements. The site operator should not use information from a support message for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis or permission.

Sharing and disclosure

NexaTools does not sell the contents of text or files that you process locally in your browser. Technical information may be handled by hosting, security, email, font, library or API providers to the extent needed for them to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed if required by a valid legal request or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or the rights and safety of others. The actual providers used should be listed here if the site later adds services that collect or receive personal information.

Data retention

Locally processed tool inputs are ordinarily held in your browser while you use the relevant page; the site does not provide a user account or cloud workspace in this release. Your browser may retain downloaded files or data you choose to save. Emails and hosting logs may remain with the relevant email or hosting provider according to its settings and policies. The site operator should periodically review support messages and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless retention is required to resolve an issue or comply with law.

Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to keep the website, email account and any operational records secure. No website, browser, email service or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You can help protect your information by keeping your browser updated, avoiding shared-device storage of sensitive files, and not sending confidential information through ordinary email.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to use a feature, avoid entering sensitive information, clear browser-stored data, manage cookies through your browser and decide whether to contact the site. If you have a privacy question or want to ask about information contained in an email you sent, contact mathsolverpro1@gmail.com. Include enough context for the operator to identify the message, but do not send passwords or identity documents in an ordinary email. Any request will be considered in light of applicable law and the information actually held by the site and its providers.

Children and general audience

NexaTools is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to request sensitive personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the site, contact the email address below so the operator can review the matter. The site operator should add any age-specific rules or notices required by the laws that apply to its audience and location.

International processing

Hosting, email, font, library and API providers may operate servers in countries other than the country from which you visit NexaTools. As a result, technical information or messages may be processed outside your country. The safeguards and terms offered by each provider apply to its processing.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised when the website changes, new tools or providers are introduced, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page. The “Last updated” date should be changed whenever a substantive revision is made. If a change materially affects how information is handled, the site should provide an appropriate notice.

Contact

For questions, privacy requests or concerns about this policy, email mathsolverpro1@gmail.com. You can also visit the Contact & Support page for the available support routes.
