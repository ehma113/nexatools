PDF Splitter

Extract selected pages from a PDF.

Free to useRuns in your browserNo account needed

Use the tool

Page numbers start at 1. Processing happens in your browser.

A QUICK GUIDE

Extract only the pages you need

Create a smaller PDF containing selected pages from a larger document. Enter individual page numbers or ranges, such as 1, 3-5.

How to use it

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Enter page numbers or ranges, separated by commas.
  3. Select Extract pages and save the new PDF.
Helpful tip

Page numbers start at 1, not 0. Preview the selected range and make sure you haven't left out a page you need.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand page ranges, page numbering and the files this tool can process.

Do page numbers start at zero?
No. Enter page numbers as they appear in the document, starting at page 1.
Can I select a range?
Yes. Use a hyphen, such as 4-8, and separate multiple selections with commas.
Can I extract pages in a different order?
Enter the page numbers in the order you want them in the output. Repeated page numbers may be included more than once.
