Age Calculator

Calculate age in years, months and days.

Free to useRuns in your browserNo account needed

Use the tool

A QUICK GUIDE

Your age, calculated precisely

Enter your birth date to see your age in years, months and days. The calculation uses calendar dates rather than simply dividing the number of days by 365, so leap years and month lengths are taken into account.

How to use it

  1. Choose your date of birth.
  2. Select Calculate age.
  3. Read your age in years, months and days.
Helpful tip

The result depends on the date entered and the calendar date today. Check the date format carefully before using the result on a form.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

A few details about date formats, leap years and how the age result is calculated.

Does this calculator account for leap years?
Yes. It uses calendar date arithmetic, so February 29 birthdays and leap years are handled by the date calculation.
Can I calculate someone else's age?
Yes. Enter any valid date of birth in the past. The date is processed in your browser.
Why can age differ by one day in different places?
Local time zones can affect date boundaries. Use the date shown on your own calendar when entering the birth date.
