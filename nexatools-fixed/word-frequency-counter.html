Helpful tip

Common words such as “the” and “and” can dominate a frequency list. Decide whether to ignore stop words before interpreting the results.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand how repeated words are counted and how to interpret a frequency list.

Are common words excluded?
The tool can ignore a built-in list of common English words such as 'the' and 'and'. Turn that option off if you want every word counted.
Does it treat capitalised words as different?
No. Words are normalised to lowercase before counting.
Does it understand word roots?
No. It counts exact word forms, so 'run' and 'running' are separate entries.
