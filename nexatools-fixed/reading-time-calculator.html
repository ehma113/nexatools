A QUICK GUIDE

Estimate how long your text takes to read

Paste an article, speech or document to estimate reading time from its word count and a selected reading speed. The estimate is a guide; speaking aloud usually takes longer than silent reading.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text into the editor.
  2. Choose a reading speed in words per minute.
  3. Read the estimated time and total word count.
Helpful tip

Choose the speaking speed when estimating a presentation or speech. For a blog post, use the average silent-reading setting as a rough guide.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand the reading-speed options and why the result is an estimate.

What reading speed should I choose?
A common silent-reading estimate is around 200–250 words per minute. Speeches and presentations are usually slower; choose a pace that matches your use.
Does it count headings?
Yes. All text pasted into the editor contributes to the word count.
Is the result exact?
No. Reading speed varies by reader, subject and text difficulty, so treat the result as an estimate.
