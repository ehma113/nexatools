A QUICK GUIDE

Check whether text colours are easy to read

Choose foreground and background colours to calculate their contrast ratio. The result includes a WCAG-style AA and AAA assessment for normal and large text.

How to use it

  1. Choose the text colour and background colour.
  2. Review the contrast ratio.
  3. Use the result to adjust colours if the contrast is too low.
Helpful tip

Check the actual text size and weight as well as the ratio. A passing contrast result is one part of accessible design, not a complete accessibility audit.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn how contrast ratios are measured and what WCAG pass levels mean.

What is a contrast ratio?
It compares the relative luminance of two colours. Higher ratios generally make text easier to distinguish from its background.
What ratio is recommended for normal text?
WCAG AA guidance uses at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. AAA uses higher thresholds.
Does a passing score guarantee accessibility?
No. Contrast is one part of accessible design. Font size, focus states, labels and other factors also matter.
