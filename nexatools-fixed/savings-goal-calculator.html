A QUICK GUIDE

Turn a savings target into a monthly plan

Enter your target amount, current savings and time horizon to estimate the amount to put aside each month. The estimate does not include interest, investment returns or fees.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount you want to save.
  2. Add any savings you already have.
  3. Choose how many months you have and calculate the monthly target.
Helpful tip

If your target date is flexible, compare a few monthly saving amounts. Remember that fees, interest and changing prices can affect the final amount.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn how the monthly saving target is calculated and what the estimate does not include.

Does the result include interest?
No. It divides the remaining target by the number of months. Interest and investment returns are not included.
What if I have already saved more than my target?
The remaining amount is zero, so no additional monthly contribution is needed to reach that target.
Can I change the currency?
Use the same currency for the target and current savings. The calculator does not convert between currencies.
