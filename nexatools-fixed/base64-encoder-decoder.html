A QUICK GUIDE

Convert text to or from Base64

Encode text into Base64 or decode a Base64 string. Base64 is a way to represent bytes as text; it is not encryption and does not protect confidential information.

How to use it

  1. Paste plain text or a Base64 string.
  2. Choose Encode or Decode.
  3. Review and copy the converted output.
Helpful tip

Base64 is an encoding format, not encryption. Do not use it to protect passwords, private keys or confidential information.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand what Base64 does and why it should not be used as a security feature.

Is Base64 encryption?
No. Base64 is an encoding format and can be reversed easily. Do not use it to protect passwords or private information.
Why does the output contain letters, numbers and symbols?
Base64 uses a defined character set to represent binary data as printable text.
Why might decoding show an error?
The input may not be valid Base64 or may not represent UTF-8 text.
