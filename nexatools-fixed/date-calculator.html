Date Calculator

Find the days between dates or add days to a date.

Free to useRuns in your browserNo account needed

Use the tool

A QUICK GUIDE

Count days or move a date forward

Find the number of days between two calendar dates, or add a chosen number of days to a starting date. This can help with planning, deadlines and date comparisons.

How to use it

  1. Choose whether to add days or compare two dates.
  2. Enter the starting date and any required second date or day count.
  3. Select Calculate to see the result.
Helpful tip

For deadlines, confirm whether the organisation counts the starting day, weekends or public holidays. This calculator gives calendar-day results.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Answers about calendar days, date ranges and counting days for deadlines.

Does the result include the start date?
The date difference is the elapsed number of days between the two dates. Inclusive counting for deadlines may require adding one day.
Are leap years included?
Yes. The calculation uses calendar dates, including leap days.
Can I calculate dates in the past?
Yes. You can compare past dates or add days to a past date as well as a future one.
