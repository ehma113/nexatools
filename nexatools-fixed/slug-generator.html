Create a clean URL slug from a title

Turn a title or phrase into a lowercase, hyphen-separated slug. Slugs are commonly used in readable page URLs and content-management systems.

How to use it

  1. Enter a title or phrase.
  2. Generate the slug.
  3. Review it for clarity and copy it into your page or post settings.
Helpful tip

Keep slugs short, descriptive and readable. If a page is already published, changing its URL may require a redirect.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Answers about readable URL slugs, punctuation and search-friendly page addresses.

What makes a good URL slug?
Keep it short, descriptive and readable. Include the main topic and avoid unnecessary filler words.
Are accented letters supported?
The generator normalises common accented characters where possible and removes unsupported punctuation.
Can two pages have the same slug?
The tool does not check your website for existing slugs. Your content system may require each page slug to be unique.
