CGPA Calculator

Calculate cumulative GPA across semesters.

Free to useRuns in your browserNo account needed

Use the tool

Add each semester GPA and its credit units. CGPA is weighted by credit units.

A QUICK GUIDE

Calculate cumulative performance across semesters

Enter the GPA and credit units for each semester to calculate a cumulative weighted average. Use the same grading scale and credit-unit conventions as your institution.

How to use it

  1. Add each semester's GPA.
  2. Enter the total credit units for that semester.
  3. Calculate to get the cumulative GPA across the entries.
Helpful tip

Enter each semester GPA alongside its actual credit units. A weighted calculation is more accurate than averaging semester GPAs equally.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand weighted CGPA calculations and why credit units matter.

How is CGPA different from GPA?
GPA commonly refers to performance over a term or semester. CGPA combines performance across multiple terms.
Should I enter semester credit units or course units?
Enter the total credit units represented by each semester GPA, not the number of courses.
Can I use a 4-point scale?
This version is configured for a 5-point scale. Do not use it for a different scale without adjusting the grade-point assumptions.
