A QUICK GUIDE

Convert common measurements in seconds

Convert between supported units for length, weight and volume. Select a category, enter the amount and choose the units you want to convert between.

How to use it

  1. Choose length, weight or volume.
  2. Enter the amount and select the starting unit.
  3. Choose the target unit and press Convert.
Helpful tip

Make sure the selected units belong to the same measurement category. A number is only meaningful when its unit is correct.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn which units are supported and how the conversion values are calculated.

Which measurement categories are available?
The current converter includes length, weight and volume. Temperature and specialist scientific units are not included.
Why are results rounded?
The converter displays a practical number of decimal places. The underlying conversion uses standard unit factors.
Are US and UK gallons the same?
No. This tool uses the US liquid gallon. Check the unit definition when converting for technical or official work.
