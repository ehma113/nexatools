INFORMATION & GUIDELINES

About NexaTools

NexaTools brings practical online utilities together in one clear, easy-to-use place.

Our purpose

Everyday digital tasks should not require complicated software or a long sign-up process. NexaTools is designed to make common jobs—working with text, images, documents, calculations and planning—simpler to complete.

What you can do here

Explore a growing library of browser-based utilities, from word counting and QR-code creation to image processing, PDF tasks, date calculations and student tools. Browse the dedicated Tools directory or explore the utility hubs on the homepage.

How our tools work

Many tools process the information you enter directly in your browser. Some features load third-party JavaScript libraries or services to provide their functionality. Check the information shown on each tool page and our Privacy Policy for details relevant to your use.

Our approach

We aim to make each tool straightforward, explain what it does and be clear about its limitations. Tools are general-purpose aids; important results should be checked before you rely on them.
