A QUICK GUIDE

Replace a phrase throughout a block of text

Find a word or phrase and replace each matching occurrence. You can choose whether matching should distinguish uppercase and lowercase letters.

How to use it

  1. Paste the text to edit.
  2. Enter the phrase to find and the replacement text.
  3. Choose Replace all and review the updated text.
Helpful tip

Check the first few replacements before using the output elsewhere. A broad search term can change words you did not intend to edit.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand how matches are found and how to avoid changing text unintentionally.

Does it replace every match?
Yes. Replace all changes every matching occurrence in the input text.
Can I match uppercase and lowercase separately?
Yes. Turn on case-sensitive matching when capitalisation should affect the result.
Does it support regular expressions?
No. This tool performs literal text replacement, not regular-expression matching.
