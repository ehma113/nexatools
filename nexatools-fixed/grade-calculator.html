Grade Calculator

Use the tool

A QUICK GUIDE

Turn marks into a percentage and grade

Enter the score earned and the total possible marks. The calculator converts the score to a percentage and applies the selected general grade boundary set.

How to use it

  1. Enter the marks you earned.
  2. Enter the total possible marks.
  3. Choose the grading scale and calculate.
Helpful tip

Enter the score earned and the maximum possible score. If your school uses special grade boundaries, compare the percentage with its official policy.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn how the percentage is calculated and why grade boundaries can vary by school.

Can I use a score out of coursework marks?
Yes. Enter the score earned and the maximum possible score, such as 18 out of 25.
Are the grade boundaries official?
No. The available boundaries are general examples. Your school, examination board or lecturer may use different boundaries.
What if I enter a score greater than the total?
The calculator rejects scores outside the valid range so the percentage is not misleading.
