Study Timetable Generator

Create a simple weekly study timetable.

Free to useRuns in your browserNo account needed

Use the tool

A QUICK GUIDE

Build a practical weekly study outline

Create a simple repeating study plan from your subjects, available days and session count. Use it as a starting point and adjust it around classes, rest and other commitments.

How to use it

  1. Enter one subject per line.
  2. Choose the number of days and sessions per day.
  3. Generate the timetable and adjust the subject rotation to suit your week.
Helpful tip

Schedule demanding subjects when you are most alert, and leave room for breaks and unexpected commitments. Treat the generated plan as a draft.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn how to balance subjects, available study time, breaks and changing deadlines.

Does it know my class timetable?
No. It creates a suggested rotation from the subjects and time settings you enter.
Can I include breaks?
The generated table lists study sessions. Add breaks between sessions when you adapt the plan.
Will the plan save automatically?
No. Copy or print the result if you want to keep it after closing the page.
