A QUICK GUIDE

Measure elapsed time with laps

Start timing an activity, pause when needed and record lap times to compare sections of a session. The stopwatch is useful for practice, cooking tasks and time trials.

How to use it

  1. Press Start to begin timing.
  2. Choose Lap to record a split without stopping.
  3. Press Pause or Reset when you finish.
Helpful tip

Use the lap function to record split times without stopping the overall timer. Keep the device awake if you need to watch a long session.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn how lap times work and what happens when you pause or reset the stopwatch.

Does the stopwatch keep running in another tab?
The display may be throttled by the browser in background tabs. Keep it visible when accurate timing matters.
What does a lap record?
A lap records the elapsed time since the previous lap, along with the overall elapsed time.
Can I export my laps?
Use the Copy laps button to copy the recorded split list.
