A QUICK GUIDE

Know exactly what your text contains

Get an instant count of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs while you type. It is useful for essays, application forms, captions, articles and any task with a length requirement.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type your text in the editor.
  2. Watch the four counters update as you edit.
  3. Use the character count when a form has a strict limit.
Helpful tip

Different platforms count hyphenated words, contractions and symbols differently. Use this live count as a guide, then check the rules of the form or platform you're submitting to.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Learn how words and characters are counted and why different platforms may show slightly different totals.

Are spaces included in the character count?
Yes. The character total includes spaces, punctuation and line breaks, because they are all characters in the text.
How are words counted?
Words are separated by whitespace. Hyphenated terms and contractions are treated as one word.
Does it save my writing?
No account or upload is needed. The counter runs in your browser; copy your text before closing the page if you want to keep it.
