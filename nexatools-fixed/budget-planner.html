A QUICK GUIDE

See how your planned spending compares with income

Enter a monthly income and estimated expenses to calculate the amount left over. Choose a currency for display; the planner does not connect to bank accounts or exchange currencies.

How to use it

  1. Choose the currency you want to display.
  2. Enter your monthly income and expenses.
  3. Calculate the balance and review the categories.
Helpful tip

Use realistic take-home income and include irregular costs such as repairs, school fees or annual subscriptions. The result is a planning estimate, not a bank balance.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Understand the monthly balance, expense categories and how your figures are handled.

Does it connect to my bank?
No. All amounts are entered manually and calculations happen in your browser.
Does choosing a currency convert my money?
No. The currency choice changes the display format only; it does not apply an exchange rate.
What does a negative balance mean?
It means the expenses you entered are greater than the income entered for that month.
